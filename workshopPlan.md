# Material provided

- Ten interposer boards consisting of a Raspberry Pi Pico plugged into an interposer which can mount to a robot. The interposers will have two LEDs driven by the PWM signal to the motors, so can be used stand-alone to bench-test software, powered through the USB cable. The Interposer will pull power from the robot and power the Pico when USB is not attached.
- One to three robots for testing with interposers
- Hardware is retained by the club after the session.
- Hard copy of the workshopContent
